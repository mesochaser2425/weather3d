Weather3D – help

3D atmosphere simulation on the graphics card. The tiles on the left open the menu tabs; a second click (or Esc) hides them. The centre shows the map or the 3D view; on the right is the analysis panel (vertical cross-section, probe, stations) – the “Analysis” button or the A key. Click the map to place the probe; hover to see the values near the ground.

Map scale

≤ 40 km – microscale (thermals, single cumulus clouds, microclimate); ≤ 400 km – mesoscale (storms, breezes, foehn, valleys); larger – synoptic scale (fronts, lows, highs). Size and detail: Settings → Grid.

Where to find things

Weather – mode, current air mass, synoptic map and 7-day forecast. Atmosphere – sounding from the probe point or the domain mean, with a full-screen view. Terrain – relief, brush (height, water, surface types), surface, hydrology. Layers – all map layers in expandable groups. Climate analysis – maps and a table of months, seasons and years. Settings – place, date, grid, map overlays, saving and loading. Stations (analysis panel) – any number of measuring points with a table, meteogram and CSV export.

The synoptic model

Primitive equations on the GPU: a channel around the whole latitude circle, 75 km grid, 12 levels up to 16 km. Lows, highs and fronts form by themselves from baroclinic instability – heat and moisture exchange with land and ocean, condensation with precipitation, and convection. The domain lies inside it: air from this model enters through the boundaries, together with clouds and precipitation.
